Her Story

My name is Jamaar, and Snowflake is my kitten. She is the last of her litter, which makes her extra special to me.

My Kitten and Her Personality

What She Looks Like

Snowflake has a pure, snowy-white coat, a tiny bubble-gum pink nose, and soft grey-green eyes. What makes her really stand out are her huge, bat-like pink ears that she hasn't quite grown into yet and her impressively long white whiskers.

What Makes Her Special

She is equal parts curious explorer and sweet cuddle-bug. Her funniest habit is getting the sudden 3 AM zoomies, crab-walking sideways with her tail puffed up before pouncing on invisible shadows.

A Day with Snowflake

Her Daily Routine

  1. Morning wake-up call: Tiny paws patting my face, loud purring, and nose boops right at sunrise.
  2. Breakfast: Chowing down eagerly on her kitten food (and occasionally getting a tiny dab of it on her pink nose).
  3. Afternoon nap in her favorite spot: Curled up into a tight, fluffy white crescent in the warmest patch of sunlight she can find.
  4. Evening playtime: Chasing toys across the floor, practicing her sneak attacks with her siblings, and leaping high into the air.

Her Favorite Things

  • Favorite toy: Crinkle balls, feather wands, and random cardboard boxes.
  • Favorite treat: Freeze-dried chicken or tuna treats.
  • Favorite napping spot: A cozy blanket or right on top of a warm laptop keyboard.

Fun Fact

Snowflake was the last kitten of her litter. Even though she arrived last, she has the biggest ears and the boldest personality of the bunch. She's always the first one to investigate a new noise!
